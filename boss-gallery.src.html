Zone 1 Boss Gallery

The eight floor bosses of One Life: Ascend in their Codex-painted frames: a four-step move, the attack wind-up and strike, two poses for the signature move, and a back view for the bosses that can turn away. Shown at three times game size.

Move loops the four steps (frames 1, 5, 2, 6). Attack holds the wind-up while the red marker would fill, then plays the strike. Special plays the boss's own move, such as Gnawbelly digging in and bursting out. Back shows the boss from behind, as it looks when it heads up the screen after you. Full cycle runs them all in turn.
